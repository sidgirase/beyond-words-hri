# Pre-proposal idea analysis (2026-09-10)

Raw files: `../proposal/Idea Analysis.md` (LLM-generated ranking) and `../proposal/Project Ideas.docx`
(the five original questions). Written before the proposal; the team chose a descendant of **Idea 3**
("when intent is ambiguous: assume, ask, or adaptively ask?"), which became [[proposal-beyond-words]].

## Still-relevant takeaways
- **Design warning** — Mannem, Macke, Stone & Mirsky, Humanoids 2023: interruptions had a *small*
  objective cost but a *sharp* drop in perceived helpfulness → treat perception measures as primary DVs,
  not just task time. Applies directly to [[study-design]].
- KnowNo (Ren et al., CoRL 2023) minimizes "help rate" as a proxy for human cost but never validated it
  with humans — part of the gap the proposal cites.
- **Windows 11 risk:** many robot simulators are Linux/macOS-only; "the characteristic failure mode is six
  weeks lost to MuJoCo/Unity". Recommendation: decide early whether an embodied simulator is in scope and
  don't revisit. Relevant to [[stretch-3-robot]].
- Midpoint (2026-10-20) needs something showable; 250 of 400 points come from check-ins/presentations.
  See [[timeline]].
- Citation hygiene notes (verified author lists/venues) live in the raw file; consult before the final report.
